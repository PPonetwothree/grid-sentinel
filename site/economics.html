Customer value

Is it worth €20k to the DSO?

Return = expected avoided loss + operational savings − fee. In the briefing's base case the value is about a third of the fee. The case works at high-risk sites, where false call-outs are frequent, or where compliance and crew safety carry a premium.

Value per site-year vs the fee

Hypothetical inputs

Don't present "one avoided €10m incident ≈ 500 site-years of fees" as a payback. It is €10m ÷ €20k, a comparison. It ignores how often such incidents happen and whether earlier verification would have changed the outcome.

Easiest value to prove in a pilot

Fewer unnecessary call-outs and a faster time to a verified decision. Avoided incidents are too rare to show within a pilot.
