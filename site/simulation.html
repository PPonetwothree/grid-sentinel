Why the rules come first

Most nuisance alarms have a simple cause the site can see for itself: wind moves many panels together, rain is broadband noise, a crew checked in at the gate. Explaining these with plain rules is cheap and auditable, and it keeps robot trips rare.

Why rules never get the last word

Run "Intruder in a storm". Wind explains the fence movement, but it cannot explain a warm, upright shape on thermal, so the guard on the wind rule lets the event through to fusion. This is the hard case the classifier must get right.

What the robot never does

It never enters a hatched clearance zone, never moves while a crew is on site, and never switches equipment. If it can't go, the operator is told why and falls back to today's process: crew or police.
