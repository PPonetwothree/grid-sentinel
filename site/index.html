Cue-triggered verification for unmanned substations

Detection is not the bottleneck. Verification is.

A fence alarm fires at 3 a.m. at an unmanned 110/20 kV yard. Was it the wind, a fox, or someone with a can of petrol? Grid Sentinel is our concept for answering that fast and safely: cheap sensors on every asset, AI at the site that rules out the harmless causes, and a docked robot that goes to look only when something stays unexplained. A human always makes the call.

Run the live substation See the engineering

Sense everywhere, move rarely. Sensors are cheap and robots are expensive, so robot time goes only where the edge AI cannot explain a signal.

The problem

Alarms at unmanned sites are ambiguous, and confirming them is slow and dangerous

Europe's ~2,500 distribution system operators (DSOs) run thousands of fenced HV/MV yards with nobody on site, watched remotely from a control room. When a sensor trips at night, the operator has two bad options.

Wait

  • Risk that it was real
  • The outage itself confirms the attack

Send a crew

  • Tens of minutes to hours at night
  • Costly call-out
  • Crew walks into a live HV yard that may have been tampered with
Our thesis is unvalidated. The threat is verified. Whether verification is the real bottleneck is the first thing we test: 15–20 interviews across three or more DSOs, plus one DSO's alarm logs.

Decision path

Every signal takes one of four exits, and the operator is always told

Benign signals are logged, low scores are watched, and unexplained ones go to the robot. If the robot can't safely go, the operator is alerted anyway.

A worked incident

02:47 to 02:51: four minutes from sensor to verified decision

The timings are assumptions for the simulation to measure, not field results. The 0.82 score is a ranking, not an 82% probability; it becomes a probability only after calibration on real data.

Words that are not interchangeable

What Grid Sentinel does, and what it does not

Grid Sentinel detects, classifies and helps verify. People respond. Police establish who did it. Nothing here physically stops anyone.

TermMeaningWho
DetectionNoticing that something unusual happenedSensors
ClassificationDeciding what it probably was, with a confidence scoreEdge AI
VerificationConfirming with independent, better evidenceRobot + human
ResponsePolice, crew, DSO operationsHumans only
AttributionEstablishing who did it; signed evidence only helpsPolice / legal
PreventionStopping harm. Not something Grid Sentinel does.n/a
