Why a European presence matters

A European DSO won't buy from a student team in India today. We would need a European partner or presence, an experienced industry advisor, security credentials, and probably an integrator as prime contractor.

The mentor ask

One industry mentor who can open the door to a DSO's alarm logs is worth more at Stage A than any hire. It turns our biggest assumptions into measurements.

Kill criteria

What would make us change or stop

  • DSOs say verification isn't a top-three pain. Stop or pivot.
  • Alarm volumes are low. The occasional call-out is acceptable; no product.
  • Their cameras and monitoring centres already cope. Narrow to blind spots, or stop.
  • Simple rules remove most false alarms. Robots aren't needed: become a software-only product.
  • Nobody shares data or pays. Stop.
If you gave us €50k today, in 120 days we would know: whether verification is a top-three pain at three or more DSOs; real alarm volumes from at least one DSO, if we get data; whether simple rules already solve most of it; and whether robot sharing is geographically feasible.
